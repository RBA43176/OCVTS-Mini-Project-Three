The Invention of the Pancake

Pancakes have served as an essential starch-based food staple globally for thousands of years, with early forms dating back to ancient times. Different cultures have adapted this simple recipe into local variations, such as French crepes and Ethiopian injera, historically functioning as affordable and quick meals for the working class. These flat cakes maintain widespread popularity today and remain closely tied to various religious traditions and festive celebrations worldwide.
